HARIS EVALUATION — RATER INSTRUCTIONS
======================================

You are evaluating business intelligence reports generated by an AI system.
Each report responds to a real company brief.

YOU DO NOT KNOW which system generated which report (A or B).
Please evaluate each report independently on its own merits.

--- HOW TO RATE ---

For each report (A and B within each R01–R10 pair), score 4 dimensions:

1. RELEVANCE (1–5)
   Does the report address the specific business idea in the brief?
   1 = Completely off-topic / generic
   3 = Partially relevant, some useful content
   5 = Highly focused on the exact idea, audience, and market

2. COMPLETENESS (1–5)
   Does the report cover the key intelligence a founder needs?
   (funding landscape, competitors, market signals, strategic context)
   1 = Only one dimension covered, or mostly empty
   3 = 2–3 dimensions covered with real content
   5 = All 4 dimensions addressed with specific data

3. ACTIONABILITY (1–5)
   Are the insights and recommendations concrete and usable?
   1 = Vague platitudes, no specific next steps
   3 = Some specific recommendations but generic in places
   5 = Specific, evidence-backed, immediately actionable advice

4. CITATION GROUNDING (1–5)
   Are claims backed by named companies, specific numbers, or cited sources?
   1 = All assertions are unsubstantiated
   3 = Some numbers/names present but many claims uncited
   5 = All major claims cite specific companies, amounts, or data points

--- FILE FORMAT ---

Open rater_form_template.csv in Excel or any text editor.
Change R1 / R2 / R3 to your assigned rater ID.
Fill in scores (1–5) for each blind_id.
Optionally add short comments in the last column.
Save as: ratings_[your_rater_id].csv

--- EXAMPLE ROW ---

rater_id, blind_id, relevance, completeness, actionability, citation_grounding, comments
R1,       R01-A,    4,         3,             4,             3,                  "Good competitor list but lacks funding amounts"

--- IMPORTANT ---

- Do NOT discuss your ratings with other raters until all are submitted.
- Rate A and B for the same brief before moving to the next brief.
- If a report is very short or empty, score all dimensions as 1 or 2.
- You have 20 reports total (10 briefs x 2 versions each).
  Estimated time: 30–45 minutes.

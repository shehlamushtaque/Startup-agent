in Canada (Source: StatCounter, 2022). FaceFlow's mobile-first strategy can
capitalize on this trend.
3. **Competition from Established Players**: The Canadian video conferencing
market is dominated by established players like Zoom and Skype (Source:
MarketsandMarkets, 2020).

innovative features, user-friendly interface, or competitive pricing.
